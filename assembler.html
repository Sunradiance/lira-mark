THE LESSON FOR R&D

  1. Don't out-spend. Out-primitive. One correct instruction beats a million-parameter wrapper.
  2. Don't out-market. Out-falsify. They ship demos; we ship misses on disk.
  3. Don't compile yet. Stay at metal until the primitive is undeniable — then others compile to you.
  4. Niche is assembler habitat. Nobody used it until every OS kernel still spoke it under the hood.
